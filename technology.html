How BioFire Works

Flame retardant mechanism, material properties, and tuning options

Overview

BioFire is a functional biopolymer extracted from wastewater biomass (bacterial extracellular polymeric substances, EPS) and processed into a flame-retardant ingredient for polymer systems. It is designed to integrate into formulations with minimal disruption while delivering multi-mechanism fire protection.

Flame-Retardant Mechanism

BioFire works through complementary effects:

  • Condensed-phase protection forms a stabilizing char layer that insulates and slows heat and mass transfer.
  • Gas-phase dilution: releases non-toxic gases during thermal decomposition that reduce local oxygen availability and flame intensity.
  • Synergy from composition: proteins, polysaccharides, and inorganic fractions contribute to the combined response.

Material Properties

  • Bio-based, carbon-rich polymeric material
  • Thermally reactive with strong char-forming behavior
  • Compatible with thermoset systems and composite formulations
  • Designed to balance fire performance with processability

Formats

  • Dry powder for compounding and solid formulations
  • Aqueous dispersion for coatings and water-based systems

Tuning Capabilities

BioFire is not “one fixed chemistry.” Performance and handling can be tailored by:

  • Extraction & processing conditions (composition and functionality)
  • Formulation strategy & loading (fire rating targets vs. property retention)
  • Rheology and dispersion control for different manufacturing routes

Development Approach

Fire performance must be validated in the final product and use case. We co-develop formulations with partners (you) and test against the relevant standards for the target market.
