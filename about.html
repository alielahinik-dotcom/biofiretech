About BioFire

Our story, team, and mission

Company

The company was founded to address the environmental and health challenges associated with conventional flame retardants while creating value from residual biomass generated in wastewater treatment.

Team

The founding team has a background in environmental biotechnology and materials science, with experience in biopolymer extraction, characterization, and flame-retardant material development. The focus is on translating lab-scale innovation into scalable industrial materials.
